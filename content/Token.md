## Explanation

A **token** is a small piece of text that a language model processes. It can be a whole word, part of a word, a single character or even punctuation.

Before an [[LLM]] processes text, it is split into tokens using a process called [[Tokenization]]. The model then works with these tokens rather than complete sentences or words.

For example, the word "understanding" might be split into smaller pieces rather than treated as a single token.

Tokens are important because they determine how much text a model can process at once (its [[Context Window]]). They are also commonly used to measure API usage and costs.
