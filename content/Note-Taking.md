## Explanation

Note taking is the process of collecting and organising information so that it can be understood, remembered and used later.

Writing something down is not necessarily the same as learning it. To make notes useful, information needs to be processed, explained in your own words and connected to things you already know.

There are different ways to take notes depending on what you want to achieve. Some notes are mainly used to store information for later reference, while others are written to develop an understanding of a subject or to record how to perform a task.

I use [[Obsidian]] to keep my notes in Markdown files and connect them through internal links. The [[PACER Method]] is one way of thinking about different types of knowledge and how to work with them.
