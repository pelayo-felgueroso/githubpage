## Explanation

Frame rate describes how many images, or frames, a camera or video system captures or displays per second. It is usually measured in frames per second (FPS).

A higher frame rate means that more images are captured in the same amount of time. This can provide smoother motion and more frequent updates about what is happening in a scene.

In computer vision and robotics, frame rate affects how often a system receives new visual information. It is important for real-time applications such as object tracking, motion capture and robot control.

However, increasing the frame rate can also require more processing power, data bandwidth and storage.
