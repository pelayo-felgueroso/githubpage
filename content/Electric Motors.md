## Explanation

Electric motors are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical motion. They are widely used in machines, vehicles, industrial equipment and robots.

The basic principle is based on the interaction between magnetic fields and electric currents, which produces torque and causes the motor to rotate.

There are different types of electric motors, including DC motors, brushless DC motors, stepper motors and AC motors. Each type has different characteristics in terms of speed, torque, efficiency and control.

In robotics, electric motors are commonly used to drive joints and other moving parts. They can be controlled to achieve specific positions, speeds or torques.
