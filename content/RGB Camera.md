## Explanation

An RGB camera captures colour images using three channels: Red, Green and Blue.

Each pixel contains intensity values for these three channels. By combining different intensities, the camera can represent a wide range of colours.

RGB cameras are commonly used in computer vision for tasks such as object detection, image classification, object tracking and visual inspection.

However, a standard RGB camera only provides colour information. It does not directly measure the distance between the camera and the objects in the scene. Depth can sometimes be estimated using additional processing, such as stereo vision or deep learning, but this information is not directly captured by a single standard RGB camera.

For applications that require direct depth measurements, an RGB camera can be combined with other sensors or replaced by an [[RGB-D Camera]].
