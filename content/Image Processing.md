## Explanation

Image Processing is the field of working with digital images to improve them, extract information or prepare them for further analysis.

A digital image is made up of [[pixels]], each containing information about colour or intensity. By applying different operations to these pixels, we can modify the image or extract useful information from it.

Some common techniques include:

- **[[Filtering]]:** Reducing noise or highlighting specific details.
    
- **[[Thresholding]]:** Separating objects from the background based on pixel intensity.
    
- **[[Edge Detection]]:** Identifying boundaries between different regions.
    
- **[[Morphological Operations]]:** Modifying the shapes and structures of objects in an image.
    

Image Processing is closely related to [[Computer Vision]], where processed images are often used to detect, identify and understand objects.
