## Explanation

A binary image is an image where each pixel can only have one of two possible values, usually 0 and 255. These values represent black and white, respectively.

Binary images are often created by applying [[Thresholding]] to a grayscale image. Pixels above or below a selected intensity threshold are assigned to one of the two values.

They are commonly used in image processing to separate objects from their background and make further operations easier, such as [[Morphological Operations]], object detection and shape analysis.
