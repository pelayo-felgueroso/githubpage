## Explanation

A gripper is a type of [[End Effector]] used by a robot to grasp, hold and release objects.

The most common mechanical grippers use two or more fingers that move towards each other to hold an object. Other designs use vacuum suction, magnetic forces or specialised mechanisms depending on the object and the task.

The choice of gripper depends on several factors, such as the object's size, shape, weight, material and the required gripping force. For example, a parallel gripper is often suitable for objects with regular shapes, while a vacuum gripper can be useful for picking up flat objects.

Grippers can be controlled in different ways. Some use position control to open and close the fingers, while others also measure force to adjust their grip and avoid damaging the object.

They are commonly used in pick-and-place operations, assembly, packaging and material handling.
