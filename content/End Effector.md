## Explanation

The **end effector** is the part of a robot that interacts directly with the environment. It is usually attached to the end of a robot arm and is used to perform a specific task.

Depending on the application, it can be a [[Gripper]], a welding tool, a suction cup or another type of tool.

Its position and orientation are important when calculating robot movements, especially when using [[Forward Kinematics]] and [[Inverse Kinematics]].
