## Explanation

The **Jacobian Matrix** describes how the movement of a robot's joints affects the movement of its end effector.

It relates joint velocities to the linear and angular velocity of the end effector. This is useful when controlling a robot or calculating how its joints need to move to follow a certain direction.

The Jacobian is also used to identify [[Robot Singularities]], where the robot can lose the ability to move in certain directions.
