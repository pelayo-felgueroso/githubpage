## Explanation

The Field of View (FOV) describes the portion of the environment that a camera or sensor can observe. It determines how much of a scene is visible from a particular position and orientation.

For a camera, the field of view depends mainly on the sensor size and the focal length of the lens. A shorter focal length generally produces a wider field of view, while a longer focal length produces a narrower one.

The field of view can be described horizontally, vertically or diagonally, and is usually measured in degrees.

In computer vision and robotics, it is important when choosing a camera and positioning it to make sure that the objects or areas of interest are visible.
