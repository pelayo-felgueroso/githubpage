## Explanation

Focal length is a property of a camera lens that describes how strongly it converges or diverges light. It is usually measured in millimetres.

In practical terms, focal length affects the angle of view and the apparent size of objects in an image. A shorter focal length gives a wider field of view, while a longer focal length gives a narrower field of view and makes objects appear larger.

In the pinhole camera model, focal length is also used to describe the relationship between a point in three-dimensional space and its projection onto the image plane.

It is one of the important parameters in camera calibration and is related to the intrinsic parameters of a camera.
