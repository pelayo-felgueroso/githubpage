## Explanation

Depth estimation is the process of determining the distance between a camera and the objects in an image. It provides information about the three-dimensional structure of a scene using visual data.

There are different ways to estimate depth. Stereo vision uses two cameras and the difference between their images to calculate distance. Other methods use RGB-D cameras, structured light, time-of-flight sensors or deep learning models that estimate depth from a single RGB image.

Depth information is useful in robotics because it helps robots understand where objects are located and how far away they are. It is also important for navigation, obstacle avoidance and object manipulation.
