## Explanation

Computer Vision is the field of technology that focuses on getting computers to interpret and understand visual information, mainly from images and videos.

The idea is to make computers extract useful information from what a camera captures. This can mean detecting objects, measuring distances, recognising patterns, tracking movement or understanding what's happening in a scene.

There are many different techniques involved, from basic image processing operations to more advanced methods based on [[Machine Learning]] and [[Deep Learning]].

For example, in an industrial application, a vision system could inspect manufactured parts, detect surface defects or measure the position of an object before a robot picks it up.

Computer Vision is closely related to [[Image Processing]], but they are not exactly the same. Image processing focuses more on modifying or analysing images, while computer vision is generally concerned with extracting information and making sense of them.
