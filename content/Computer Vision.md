## Explanation

Computer Vision is a field of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on enabling computers to extract information from images and videos. The goal is to make machines interpret visual information and use it to understand their surroundings or perform specific tasks.

It starts with [[Imaging]], which explains how light interacts with surfaces and how cameras capture it to form images. Once an image has been captured, different techniques can be used to process and analyse its content.

[[Image Processing]] focuses on modifying or improving images to make them more useful. This includes operations such as filtering, noise reduction, contrast adjustment and edge detection.

Computer Vision goes a step further by extracting meaningful information from images. For example, [[Object Detection]] identifies objects and estimates their positions, [[Image Segmentation]] separates an image into different regions, and [[Object Tracking]] follows objects across video frames.

Many of these tasks use [[Machine Learning]] and [[Deep Learning]] to recognise patterns and learn from visual data. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), for example, are widely used in image classification, object detection and segmentation.

Computer Vision is used in many areas, including robotics, autonomous vehicles, industrial inspection, medical imaging and surveillance. In robotics, it allows machines to detect objects, estimate their positions and interact with their surroundings.
