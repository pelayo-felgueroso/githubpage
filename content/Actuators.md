## Explanation

Actuators are components that convert energy into physical movement. They are used in machines and robots to move parts, apply forces or control mechanisms.

Depending on how they work, actuators can be electric, pneumatic or hydraulic. Electric actuators use [[Electric Motors]] or other electrical mechanisms, while pneumatic and hydraulic actuators use pressurised air or liquid.

In robotics, actuators are responsible for moving the [[Robot Joints]], allowing the robot to change its position and orientation. Their characteristics, such as speed, force, torque and precision, influence how the robot can move and interact with its environment.
