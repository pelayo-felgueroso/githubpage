## Explanation

Generative AI is a branch of [[Artificial Intelligence]] focused on creating new content based on patterns learned from training data. It can generate text, images, audio, video and other types of information.

These systems learn how data is structured and use that knowledge to produce new outputs. Examples include large language models that generate text, image generation models and systems that create actions for robots.

Many generative AI systems are based on [[Deep Learning]] architectures, such as [[Transformer]] models and diffusion models.
