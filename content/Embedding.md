## Explanation

An embedding is a way of representing information as a list of numbers (a vector) so that a computer can work with it.

The interesting thing is that these numbers can capture relationships between things. For example, words or sentences with similar meanings tend to have similar embeddings, which means they are closer to each other in a mathematical space.

This is useful for things like semantic search, where you want to find information based on what it means rather than just matching the exact words you typed.

Embeddings are also an important part of how [[LLM]]s work, and are used in things like [[RAG]] to find relevant information before generating an answer.
